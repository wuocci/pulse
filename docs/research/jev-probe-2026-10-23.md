# Jev probe, 2026-10-23

Two throwaway scripts ran `jev-latest` (TypeSafe) against live data to answer: does it work on our Sources, and what does it cost? The scripts are not committed.

## Cost and speed

| Source | Items | Tokens per item | Cost per 1000 items | Latency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HSL alerts (Finnish text) | 24 | ~720 | $0.030 | 170–400 ms |
| HSL alerts (English text) | 24 | ~670 | $0.028 | same |
| Helsinki excavation notices | 40 | ~600 | $0.026 | ~190 ms |

Pricing is $0.042 per million input tokens; output is free. Cost is not a constraint. Many notices share identical text (22 of 40 were "Pohjatutkimus: koekuopat"), so caching by text hash cuts it further.

## HSL alerts

- **Lines:** precision 89–92% when choosing among line names found in the text. Recall looked low only because 100 of the 135 lines HSL lists never appear in the alert text (e.g. a platform closure lists 45 lines without naming them). Digitransit gives affected lines and stops as structured data, so **HSL needs no Classifier for lines**.
- **Effect and severity:** 42% and 58% agreement with HSL's own labels, but those labels are inconsistent. A closed platform is labelled `DETOUR`, possible delays `OTHER_EFFECT`, and the same detour appears once as INFO and once as WARNING. Jev's choices were often more precise. HSL labels are not a usable answer key.
- Language made no difference to accuracy; English used ~8% fewer tokens.

## Helsinki excavation notices

- **Kind of work** (water/sewer, district heating, electricity/telecom, paving, street construction, building site, other): agreed with a keyword guess on all 11 notices that had one, and was right where keywords failed (word forms like "kaukolämmön"). Confidence drops to ~0.5 on genuinely vague text, which suits "low confidence → show, don't push".
- **Current end date from the change log** (choice over dates found by regex): correct on all 6 notices with dates. Twice it disagreed with the city's structured `tyo_paattyy`, and both times the text supported Jev: once the text says the end moved to 29.08.2027 while the field says 29.09.2027, once the only date was the note's own date and Jev answered "not stated". **When text and field disagree, keep both and flag the conflict.**
- **Questions the text can't answer** (does it affect traffic, how much does it disturb residents) got middling answers (0.3–0.6, scores 1.1–2.3) with no real signal. Traffic impact should come from Helsinki's temporary traffic arrangement layers instead.

## Consequences

- ADR 0003 amended: the Classifier chooses among candidates and is used only where a Source lacks structure.
- Issue #10 updated with this scope; #2 and #7 note what changes for them.
